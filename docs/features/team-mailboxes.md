# Team mailboxes

In a mailbox your team shares, such as `info@` or `support@`, you can talk about a message right beside it, without leaving Bulwark. Only your team sees these notes; the customer never does.

Your admin turns this on. If you see **Note** next to Reply, Reply all and Forward in a shared mailbox, it is on.

## Write a note

1. Open the message.
2. Click **Note**. A field opens at the end of the conversation.
3. Type, and press Enter. Shift+Enter starts a new line.

The note appears as a yellow card between the messages, with your name and picture. Everyone in the team sees it within seconds. Escape closes the field; what you typed stays there for later.

Above the message a line shows how many notes there are and the newest one. Click it to go to the notes.

In the list, a speech bubble shows how many notes a conversation has. A dot next to it means there is a note you haven't seen yet.

A note that couldn't be saved stays in the conversation with the reason and **Retry**. **Edit** puts its text back into the field.

To delete one of your own notes, click the bin on its card. **Undo** in the message at the bottom brings it back. You can't change a note; delete it and write a new one. You can't delete other people's notes.

## Mention someone

Type @ in a note and pick a colleague from the list (with the arrow keys and Enter, or by tapping). They get a notification: "Mabel mentioned you in “Offer for the Mulberry project”". Nobody else is notified about the note.

## Assign and mark done

- **Assign to me** takes a message.
- **Assign to…** gives it to someone: pick their name.
- **Unassign** takes the name off again.
- **Mark done** when it's dealt with, **Reopen** when it isn't after all.

You find these in the reader's toolbar, in the message's right-click menu, in the buttons that show when you point at a row, and on phones in the row menu (touch and hold a message) and the reader's **More** menu. Each change leaves a grey line in the conversation, such as "Linus assigned this to Josh".

List rows show who has a message and a **Done** mark.

## Filters

Above the list of a team mailbox's folder:

- **Open**: everything not marked done.
- **Assigned to me**: your work.
- **Mentions me**: conversations with a note that mentions you.
- **With notes**: conversations that have notes.

Click a filter to show only those messages, and click it again to show everything.

## If something doesn't work

- **"You can read notes here but not write them."** You can read this mailbox but not change it, so there is no Note button. You still see the team's notes.
- **"Notes can't be saved in this mailbox. Ask your admin."** The server refused the note. Your admin can give you the rights to write in the shared mailbox.
- **"You're offline."** Notes are written when you're back online.
- **"This mailbox is full. Ask your admin."** The shared mailbox has no room left.

In other mail apps (Thunderbird, Outlook, your phone's mail app) the notes are in a folder called **Team notes** of the shared mailbox.
