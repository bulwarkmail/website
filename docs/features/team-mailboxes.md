# Team mailboxes

In a mailbox your team shares, such as `info@` or `support@`, you can talk about a message right beside it, without leaving Bulwark. Only your team sees these notes; the customer never does.

Your admin turns this on. If a shared mailbox shows a yellow note line above the reply box, it is on.

## Write a note

1. Open the message.
2. Click the note line above the reply box ("Note for your team, @ to mention"), or **Note** next to Reply, Reply all and Forward.
3. Type, and press Enter. Shift+Enter starts a new line.

The note appears as a yellow card between the messages, with your name and picture. Everyone in the team sees it within seconds. Escape leaves the note line; what you typed stays there for later. **Cancel** empties it.

Above the message a line shows how many notes there are and the newest one. Click it to go to the notes.

In the list, a speech bubble shows how many notes a conversation has. A dot next to it means there is a note you haven't seen yet.

A note that couldn't be saved stays in the conversation with the reason and **Retry**. **Edit** puts its text back into the note line.

To delete one of your own notes, click the bin on its card. **Undo** in the message at the bottom brings it back. You can't change a note; delete it and write a new one. You can't delete other people's notes.

## Mention someone

Type @ in a note and pick a colleague from the list (with the arrow keys and Enter, or by tapping). The list shows your team first, then the rest of your organisation's directory where your admin allows that. The colleague gets a notification: "Mabel mentioned you in “Offer for the Mulberry project”". The message is assigned to them too, next to anyone who already has it. Nobody else is notified about the note.

Someone who isn't in the list yet shows up once they have opened the team mailbox.

## Assign and mark done

A message can be assigned to several people. Under its subject you see who has it, each with an × that takes them off again, and two buttons:

- **Assign** opens the team's list: tick everyone who should have the message and click **Save**.
- **Done** marks it dealt with. Click it again to reopen it.

The message's right-click menu, the reader's **More** menu and the buttons that show when you point at a row have the same actions: **Assign to me**, **Assign to…**, **Unassign me** (or **Unassign**, which takes everyone off), **Mark done** and **Reopen**. On phones they are in the row menu (touch and hold a message). Each change leaves a grey line in the conversation, such as "Linus assigned this to Josh".

List rows show who has a message, with "+1" and so on when several people have it, and a **Done** mark.

## Filters

Above the list of a team mailbox's folder:

- **Open**: everything not marked done.
- **Assigned to me**: your work.
- **Mentions me**: conversations with a note that mentions you.
- **With notes**: conversations that have notes.

Click a filter to show only those messages, and click it again to show everything.

## If something doesn't work

- **"You can read notes here but not write them."** You can read this mailbox but not change it, so there is no note line. You still see the team's notes.
- **"Notes can't be saved in this mailbox. Ask your admin."** The server refused the note. Your admin can give you the rights to write in the shared mailbox.
- **"You're offline."** Notes are written when you're back online.
- **"This mailbox is full. Ask your admin."** The shared mailbox has no room left.

In other mail apps (Thunderbird, Outlook, your phone's mail app) the notes are in a folder called **Team notes** of the shared mailbox.
